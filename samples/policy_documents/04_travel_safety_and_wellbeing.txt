Guidance 1.0. Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. Applies to all business travel.

Travel advisories
-----------------

Check the Travel Desk advisory page before booking. Trips to destinations marked "High risk" need
written approval from the Head of People and a safety briefing call before departure.

Rest after overnight travel
---------------------------

After an overnight flight, employees should not be scheduled for meetings within 8 hours of arrival.
Managers plan agendas around this rest period.

Late arrivals
-------------

For arrivals between 22:00 and 06:00 local time, use a pre-booked airport transfer or an app-based
taxi rather than street taxis. The normal local transport rules of the travel policy apply to the
cost.

Choosing accommodation
----------------------

Choose hotels with a 24-hour front desk and in-room safe. Solo travellers may ask the Travel Desk
for a room that is not on the ground floor.

Incident reporting
------------------

Report any accident, theft, harassment or health incident during a trip to your manager and to the
People team within 24 hours, using the "Travel Incident" form on the intranet.
