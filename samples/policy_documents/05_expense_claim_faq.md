FAQ 1.0. Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. Answers to common questions about travel expense claims.

### How do I check a claim before I submit it?

Open Claims & Verification in the Expense Policy Assistant, enter one line per hotel night, flight journey or local transport charge, and select Assess claim. The check uses the same caps as Finance.

### Which documents do I need?

Itemised hotel invoices, flight invoices with boarding passes, and taxi or transit receipts. Without them the claim is incomplete even if an amount can be calculated.

### What happens if part of my claim is over the cap?

The amount over the cap is shown separately and the claim goes to a human reviewer. The assistant never approves or rejects a claim.

### Can I claim the cost of airline miles or hotel points?

No. You may keep miles and points earned on business trips, but they cannot be claimed as an expense.

### I lost a receipt. What should I do?

Ask the merchant for a duplicate invoice first. If that is not possible, raise a ticket in the "Travel Claims" category; a Finance reviewer decides how to proceed. The assistant cannot accept a claim line without its evidence.

### Who do I contact with questions?

Raise a ticket in the "Travel Claims" category on the intranet. Replies are sent within 2 working days.
