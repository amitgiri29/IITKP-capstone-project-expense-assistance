Guidance 1.0. Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31. Applies to employees who attend external conferences, summits and trade shows. It supplements the travel policy and does not change any spending cap.

## Pre-approval for conference registration

Conference registration fees must be pre-approved by the employee's department head at least 14 calendar days before the event starts. Requests are raised in the "Conference Request" form with the event name, dates, agenda link and the expected business outcome. Registrations paid without pre-approval are not reimbursed.

## Early-bird registration

Employees should book the early-bird ticket whenever it is available. If an early-bird ticket was missed, the employee must explain why in the claim notes.

## Hotel choice for conferences

Prefer the official conference partner hotel when its nightly rate is within your hotel cap. Otherwise choose a hotel within 3 km of the venue. The normal policy hotel caps still apply.

## Trip report

Within 10 working days after returning, attendees share a one-page trip report on the internal "Learning Hub" page, covering three key takeaways and any useful contacts made at the event.

## Team attendance limit

No more than three employees from the same team may attend the same conference, unless the department head approves an exception in writing.
