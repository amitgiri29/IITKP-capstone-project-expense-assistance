Rule P01 | Scope and dates
Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31

Fictional company SPARKS AI Lab. Policy version 1.1 applies to expense service dates from 1 January 2026 through 31 December 2026 inclusive. India is the home country. Grade E means employee and grade M means manager; an unknown grade requires clarification. No policy is established outside these dates.
