Rule P09 | Evidence and confirmation
Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31

Itemized hotel invoices, flight invoices plus boarding passes, and taxi or transit receipts are required for claim completion. Missing evidence means NEEDS_EVIDENCE even if an indicative amount can be calculated. Any amount extracted from an image is provisional until the user confirms critical fields. This is a document completeness check, not a verification that the receipt is genuine.
