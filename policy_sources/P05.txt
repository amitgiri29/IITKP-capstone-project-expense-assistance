Rule P05 | International flights
Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31

Economy only for both grades. All inclusive cap per traveler per one way journey after documented conversion to INR: E 80000 and M 120000. Includes one checked bag up to 20 kg, taxes and mandatory fees. Round trips are assessed per direction. Flight duration does not itself permit a cabin upgrade. Missing baggage or mandatory-fee amounts make search offers provisional.
