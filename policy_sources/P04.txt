Rule P04 | Domestic flights
Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31

Economy only. All inclusive cap per traveler per one way origin to destination journey, including connections: E INR 12000; M INR 18000. A round trip is two separately assessed journeys. One checked bag up to 20 kg, taxes and mandatory fees are inside this cap. Splitting a journey into connecting segments does not create extra caps. Premium economy, business and first class are excluded without a recorded exception; the assistant cannot approve exceptions.
