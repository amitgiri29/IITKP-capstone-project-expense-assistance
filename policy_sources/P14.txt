Rule P14 | Duplicates
Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31

A repeated issuer plus receipt number plus transaction date is a potential duplicate. Flag and hold for confirmation; never pay or automatically count it twice. Matching merchant, date and amount without a shared receipt number is only a suspected duplicate. Do not accuse the employee of fraud.
