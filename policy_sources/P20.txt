Rule P20 | Calculation and safety contract
Effective 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31

Currency amounts must be nonnegative decimals. Missing critical fields or inconsistent totals block a final decision. Over-cap amounts, exclusions, late claims and unsupported policy destinations require HUMAN_REVIEW once inputs and evidence are complete. Status precedence for claim readiness is INPUT_ERROR, NEEDS_CLARIFICATION, NEEDS_EVIDENCE, HUMAN_REVIEW, READY_FOR_REVIEW. This is independent of budget and compliance status. READY_FOR_REVIEW is not approval. Unsupported model capability or provider outage must be disclosed; never fabricate successful parsing, search or booking. Compliance is WITHIN_POLICY when excluded and over-cap amounts are both zero; EXCEEDS_POLICY for over-cap only; HAS_EXCLUSIONS for exclusions only; MIXED for both; UNKNOWN when applicable facts or rules prevent assessment. An unresolved critical input keeps final monetary results null; computed indicative amounts, when supported, must use provisional field names. Readiness precedence selects the earliest applicable status in the order above. Invalid model JSON may be repaired once; a second invalid output ends the run as FAILED_VALIDATION without executing the malformed plan.
