Use case UC01 — Answering a policy question

One model call per question. Python owns every number and checks every citation; the model writes the explanation.

SPARKS AI LAB
1

Ask

The traveler asks in Policy Assistant, the Home ask box or the top bar.

Streamlit
2

Authorize

Bearer token resolves the server-held session: organization, grade, role. Rate limit applied.

FastAPI · SQLite
3

Guard

Requests for secrets, bookings or another person's data are refused here.

Refused → no model call
4

Applicability

Grade, country, city and service date from the question, the session and a city gazetteer.

Outside policy dates → answered without the model
5

Retrieve

Keyword (BM25) and semantic (vector) search over policy and library sections, filtered by organization, date and geography, fused by rank. Top 6 passages.

6

Registry facts

The exact cap, currency and unit for the grade and place come from the reviewed rule registry.

Domain core · Decimal

Structured answer

Claude receives the question, verified facts and evidence (marked as data) and returns JSON: answer, citations, facts, missing fields.

Claude Sonnet 5.5 · JSON schema
8

Reconcile

Registry numbers override the model's. Citations not retrieved are removed. Never approves exceptions or invents FX; unsupported destinations go to human review.

9

Cited answer

Answer, limit badge, clarification or review card, and expandable policy sources with rule IDs.
